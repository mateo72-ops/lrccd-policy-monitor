BUDGET PLANNING AND ADMINISTRATION 
 
Revolving Cash Funds P-8152 
Funds 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Revolving Cash Funds   
1.1 The Board of Trustees authorizes revolving cash funds in amounts not to exceed 
the maximum permitted by law. 
1.2 These funds shall be used by authorized District employees to carry out District 
business in accordance with the California Community Colleges Chancellor's 
Office Budget and Accounting Manual.
