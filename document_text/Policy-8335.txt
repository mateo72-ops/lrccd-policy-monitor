Consulting Services P-8335 
Business Services 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Consulting Services and Personal Services 
1.1 The District may contract for special services or consultants to bring specific 
knowledge or expertise to an issue, project or activity under study. 
2.0 Authorization to Obtain Consulting Services 
2.1 Consulting services require authorization by one of the signatories listed in R-
8315. No work may be performed in advance of such authorization. 
2.2 A request for proposals shall be developed and sent to qualifying interested 
contractors and/or professionals in accordance with applicable procurement 
regulations and district practices.
