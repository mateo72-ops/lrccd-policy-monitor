1.0 Conversion to Individual Policy 
1.1 Present insurance carriers may allow an employee who is terminating to have the 
option of converting to a personal policy and paying the premiums designated by 
the insurer to the insurance company directly. 
1.2 Insurance carriers may allow an employee who is resigning to convert the 
District-paid policy to a personal one. Individual arrangements must be worked 
out with the carrier.
