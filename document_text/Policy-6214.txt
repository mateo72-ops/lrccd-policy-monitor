CLASSIFICATION PLAN 
 
Membership in the Classified Service P-6214 
Authorization for Plan 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Membership (Educ. Code 88003; Educ. Code 88004) 
1.1 Employees in the service of the District who are employed to work on a 
continuing basis in regularly authorized positions not requiring certification 
qualifications, whether permanent or probationary, full- or part-time, are members 
of the classified service. 
1.2 Exclusions. The following are excluded from membership in the classified 
service: 
1.2.1 Employees holding positions that require certification qualifications; 
1.2.2 consultants and technical experts employed by the District on a temporary 
basis;  
1.2.3 substitute and short-term employees who work less than seventy-five 
percent (75%) of the fiscal year (195 days); 
1.2.4 persons training in apprentice positions; 
1.2.5 full-time or part-time students employed in work study student help or 
work experience education programs conducted by the District.
