ABSENCES AND LEAVES  Required Court Appearance  R-6433 
Notification and Absence Reporting 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Required Court Appearance 
1.1 A copy of the subpoena must be attached to the report of absence form submitted 
by the employee. 
1.2 The employee is expected to return to work whenever it is not necessary to be 
absent the entire day.
