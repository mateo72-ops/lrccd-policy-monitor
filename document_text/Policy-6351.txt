Public Employees Retirement

P-6351 
Retirement Programs 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Authorization 
1.1 Classified employees, as qualified by PERS, shall maintain membership in the 
State Retirement System. 
1.2 The District will contribute toward the employee's service retirement allowance at 
the rate determined by the retirement system. 
1.3 A deduction consistent with the rates established by PERS will be made from the 
employee's monthly warrant.
