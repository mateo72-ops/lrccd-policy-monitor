1.0 Equal Opportunity 
1.1 It is the intent of the Los Rios Community College District to make its business 
procedures conform to the practices of a model government entity to the extent 
feasible. 
1.2 The District shall attempt, in all areas, to fulfill by its own example the principles 
that it seeks in others with whom it does business. 
2.0 Purchasing of Supplies and Equipment 
2.1 The matter of District spending, including the purchase of supplies and 
equipment, is one to which the Los Rios Community College District Board of 
Trustees gives most critical attention. 
2.2 The Board of Trustees seeks in every case to purchase, for the least expenditure of 
funds, optimum quality for the function to be served. 
2.3 The Board of Trustees is guided by appropriate legal provisions for purchase of 
supplies and equipment, and for work to be done. (Ed. Code, §§ 81641-81656 and 
Pub Cont. Code, § 20651) 
2.4 Other things being equal, purchases shall be made within the area served by the 
District. 
3.0 Bidding and Quotation Procedure 
3.1 Where it is legally required or if it is determined by the Vice Chancellor, Finance 
and Administration to be in the best interest of the District to do so, supplies and 
work to be done shall be purchased on a competitive bid or quotation basis.
