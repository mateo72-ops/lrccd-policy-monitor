BUILDING CONSTRUCTION PROGRAM 
 
Facility Planning P-8417 
General Planning 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Staff Participation 
1.1 It is the policy of the District to encourage maximum participation of all 
college personnel in planning for construction and alteration of facilities, 
so that buildings and grounds may best reflect the needs of the educational 
program of the college. 
1.2 Suggested changes in the design of a facility project that is under 
development shall be presented to the designated college coordinator.  The 
request shall be reviewed prior to submitting the recommendation to the 
District's facility planning staff.  
1.3 Suggested changes in the design of a facility project that is under 
construction shall be presented to the designated college administrator, or 
the district facility planning staff for consideration.  The impact of the 
proposed change shall be evaluated considering education program needs, 
cost considerations and construction timelines or delays. 
2.0 The District's education plan(s) or specific program plans shall be 
an integral part of the District's facility planning activity. 
3.0 The Chancellor shall develop facilities planning regulations.
