1.0 Copyright 
1.1 Computer programs are classified as "literary works." As such, they are covered 
under copyright protection (17 USC Section 101). Copyright extends to computer 
programs in source code as well as object code. Even though the copyright notice 
is not displayed, a computer program may still be under copyright protection. 
1.2 All students, faculty, and staff shall follow the Los Rios Community College 
District copyright Policy P-8333 and Administrative Regulation R-8333, Federal, 
state and local law concerning copyright of computer programs.
