1.0 Programs and Services for Students with Disabilities 
1.1. The Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (ADA) in conjunction with Section 504 
of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 prohibits entities from excluding individuals from 
jobs, services, activities or benefits based upon disability. The intent of the ADA is to 
dispel stereotypes and assumptions about disabilities and ensure equal opportunity 
and encourage full participation for persons with disabilities. 
1.2. In compliance with these regulations, the Los Rios Community College District has 
implemented an Office of Programs and Services for Students with Disabilities at 
each of the District's colleges to coordinate the provision of appropriate academic 
adjustments to students eligible for services. Eligibility for program participation is 
determined in accordance with guidelines established through the ADA, Section 504 
and Title 5 of the California Code of Regulations.
