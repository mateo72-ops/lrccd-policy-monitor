1.0  Student Financial Aid Programs 
1.1 It is the policy of the Los Rios Community College District to provide financial 
aid as a means of removing financial barriers to education for students who 
qualify for assistance to meet the cost of attendance as defined by the college.   
Increased student access to the District colleges and increased student retention 
are primary objectives of the Student Financial Aid program.
