1.0 Authority 
1.1 Authority to impose conditions and regulations governing vehicles and to regulate 
traffic and parking on the property of the Los Rios Community College District 
rests with the Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees. 
1.2 The Chancellor or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations to 
implement this District Policy.
