1.0 Conditions Regulating Absences 
1.1 Unauthorized absences will result in a reduction in compensation and may result 
in additional discipline. 
1.2 An employee shall not be allowed to undertake any gainful employment while 
absent, except during vacation, unless specifically authorized by the Board
