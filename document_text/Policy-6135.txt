EMPLOYMENT PROCEDURES 
 
Demotion P-6135 
Assignment and Reassignment   1 of 1 
 
1.0 Purpose 
1.1 The demotion of a classified employee shall be for the purpose of (1) providing 
the best possible support services for the educational program, (2) assisting an 
employee to make necessary adjustments in the responsibility level of his/her 
work, or (3) discipline. 
 (Formerly R-6125)
