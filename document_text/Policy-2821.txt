1.0 Exempt Criteria 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District shall establish criteria to exempt certain 
students from participation in one or more components of the Student Success and 
Support Program requirements of orientation, assessment, counseling, advising, or 
student education plan development. (Title 5, § 55532)
