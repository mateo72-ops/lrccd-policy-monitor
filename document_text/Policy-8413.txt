Site Acquisition Procedures P-8413 
General Planning 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Negotiation Period 
1.1 A period of ninety (90) days shall be established as the negotiating period for 
acquisition of real property for the Los Rios District. 
1.2 If the negotiations are not proceeding to a satisfactory conclusion by the end of 
the 90-day period, a recommendation will be presented to the Board as to further 
steps to be taken in acquiring the site.
