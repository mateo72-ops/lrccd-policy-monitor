1.0 Replacement or Repair of Employees’ Personal Property 
1.1 The District may pay for the cost of replacing or repairing property necessarily 
worn by an employee when such items are damaged in the line of duty without 
fault or negligence on the part of the employee. 
1.2 Such items may include but are not limited to eye glasses, dentures, hearing aids 
or watches. 
2.0 Payment of Claims 
1.3 Payment shall be subject to the availability of funds authorized by the Board of 
Trustees for this specific purpose.
