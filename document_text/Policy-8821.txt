ADMINISTRATIVE COMPUTER USE

1.0 Access 
1.1 No person shall have access to the Los Rios Community College District Systems 
unless authorized by a District Administrator in the manner set forth in these 
Policies and Administrative Regulations. 
2.1 Users should be provided with access to a copy of these policies and regulations. 
3.1 Designated administrators may develop additional procedures for use of the 
applicable computer system.
