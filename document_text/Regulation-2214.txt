1.0 International Students 
1.1 Requirements for Admissions 
1.1.1 General requirements for admission are: 
1.1.1.1 The equivalent of a U.S. high school diploma (12 years of 
education) with a G.P.A. of 2.0 or above, or equivalent;  
1.1.1.2 a score as determined and published  by the college on the test of 
English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) and/or a college specific 
English as a second language assessment; 
1.1.1.3 a financial resource certification; 
1.1.1.4 a tuberculin clearance examination prior to registration. 
Tuberculosis tests will be given at campus health centers; 
1.1.1.5 proof of acceptable health insurance coverage for the period of 
time the student will be in the United States;  
1.1.1.6 a fifty dollar ($50.00) International Student Application fee 
payable at the time of application for admission; and 
1.1.1.7 approval by the BCIS (Bureau of Citizenship & Immigration 
Services) that the office of the Secretary of State has approved a 
Visa admitting the Alien to enter the United States and enroll at an 
approved institution of higher learning.  
1.1.2 High School International Students  
1.1.2.1 High school international students (F-1, J-1 or other non immigrant 
status) are admissible on an individual basis to be determined by 
the college specific Designated School Official(s) or by the 
College President. 
1.1.3  Concurrent Enrollment Students 
1.1.3.1 International students wishing to concurrently enroll at another Los 
Rios Community College are admissible on an individual basis to 
be determined by the college specific's 1-20 Designated School 
Official(s) or by the College President. 
1.1.4 For college transfers, the additional qualifications are: 
1.1.4.1 For new students transferring within the United States, students 
must be in status to be eligible for transfer.  Students must submit a 
"transfer form/letter" completed at the previous school by the
