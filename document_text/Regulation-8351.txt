Security Services R-8351 
Property Management 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Traffic Control 
1.1 Responsibility 
1.1.1 The College President is responsible for the enforcement of all rules and 
regulations established for the campus and for the enforcement of all State 
laws relating to traffic on campus. 
1.2 Traffic Regulations 
1.2.1 The College President may make rules which are deemed necessary for 
the control of traffic on the campus grounds. 
1.2.2 The College President may appropriately sign or mark any place where it 
is deemed that it is necessary to control traffic on the campus. 
1.3 Enforcement 
1.3.1 Vehicles parked in violation of any authorized rule or regulation may be 
removed by towing at the owner’s expense.
