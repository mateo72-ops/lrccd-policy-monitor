COMPENSATION AND RELATED BENEFITS 
 
Reemployment P-6328 
Compensation for Services 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Salary Placement Upon Reemployment 
1.1 Whenever a former employee is reemployed within a 12-month period in the 
same or similar positions (same salary range; same qualifications) as held at the 
time of resignation, the individual will be assigned to the same step as formerly 
held. 
1.2 Whenever a former employee is selected for a position having a higher salary 
range than the range allocation for the position previously held, the individual will 
be assigned to Step 1 of the appropriate range. 
2.0 Use of Accrued Sick Leave 
2.1 Whenever a former employee is reemployed within a 12-month period of 
termination of former services, the employee will be credited with any 
accumulated sick leave held at the time of resignation.
