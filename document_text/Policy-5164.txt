1.0  Participation in Retirement System  
1.1 All regular full-time academic employees shall be required to participate in the State 
Teachers' Retirement System (STRS) except that new employees who are already 
members of the Public Employees Retirement System (PERS) may choose between 
continuing with PERS or joining STRS. (E.C. 22608) 
2.0  Pre-Retirement Program  
2.1 Full-Time academic employees approaching retirement may apply for partial 
employment for up to five (5) years immediately preceding final retirement. In order 
to participate in this program, academic employees must meet predetermined 
requirements (E. C. 87483).
