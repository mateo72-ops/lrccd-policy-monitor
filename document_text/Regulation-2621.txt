Student Services and Scholarships 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Financial Assistance 
1.1 Los Rios Community College District students will be provided with financial 
assistance consisting of available grants, loans, work study and other such aids or 
a combination by: 
1.1.1 maintaining a match level and maintenance of effort to provide assistance 
to all eligible students; 
1.1.2 establishing a comprehensive and diverse aid program from Federal, State, 
community and private sources. 
2.0 Administrative Practices 
2.1 Administrative practices will be to: 
2.1.1 increase community awareness of services and programs offered; 
2.1.2 make every effort to meet the demonstrated needs of all eligible students 
to the extent funding will permit; 
2.1.3 exercise controls to insure that need-based aid awards do not exceed 
documented need; 
2.1.4 award aid on the basis of demonstrated financial need except where 
established otherwise in regulation; 
2.1.5 recognize that the primary responsibility for financing rests with the 
student and family. Financial assistance from the college and other sources 
is only intended as supplementary to the efforts of the family; 
2.1.6 help students seek, obtain, and make the best use of all financial resources 
available; 
2.1.7 provide literature with a clear statement of the costs of attendance; 
2.1.8 inform the student of the conditions under which an award is granted prior 
to the time when the offer is made; 
2.1.9 respect the confidentiality of student records. 
2.1.10 maintain an institutional policy and procedures manual at each college for 
all Federal and State mandated financial assistance requirements.
