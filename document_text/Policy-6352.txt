Social Security P-6352 
Retirement Programs 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Authorization 
1.1 Classified employees (hired after 1959) eligible for membership in PERS must 
become members of the Social Security System (OASDI). 
1.2 The District will contribute to the employee's old age, survivors, and disability 
insurance at a rate determined by the Social Security System. 
1.3 A deduction consistent with the rates established by Social Security will be made 
from the employee's monthly warrant.
