1.0 Definitions 
1.1 Salary Range - A series of consecutive salary steps that comprise the rate of pay 
for specific position classes. 
1.2 Salary Step - A specific location in a salary range, not a dollar amount. 
1.3 Salary Rate - A specific amount of money paid for a specified period of service, 
i.e., dollars per hour or per month
