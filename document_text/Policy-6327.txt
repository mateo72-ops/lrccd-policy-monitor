Compensation for Services 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Temporary and Substitute Employees 
1.1 Temporary and substitute employees will be paid at the hourly rate for the first step 
of the appropriate range on the classified salary schedule.  Under extenuating 
circumstances, the Chancellor/Superintendent or designee may authorize a higher 
step. 
2.0 Regular Employees 
2.1 Regular employees will be paid according to the various bargaining agreements in 
force.  (Appendices B, C and D)
