ACADEMIC STANDARDS 
 
Credit by Correspondence P-7253 
Grading Practices and Procedures 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Correspondence Course Credit 
1.1 Credit shall be granted for courses taken by correspondence supplied by the 
University of California, or any other university or college fully accredited 
provided the course or courses are included in the subject offerings in the local 
district community college, in full keeping with regular procedures of granting 
credit by California Community Colleges, and given full credit in the parent 
institution. 
1.2 Credit shall be granted for courses taken by correspondence through the Defense 
Activity for Non-Traditional Education Support (DANTES) in keeping with the 
recommendations of the American Council on Education. 
1.3 Not more than twenty (20) credit hours shall be granted for courses taken by 
correspondence.
