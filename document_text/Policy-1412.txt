USE OF SCHOOL FACILITIES 
 
Fees for Use of Facilities   P-1412 
Civic Center Activities 1 of 1 
 
1.0  Authorization 
1.1 The Chancellor of the Los Rios Community College District is authorized to 
establish a fee schedule for non-District facility use. 
1.2 The fee schedule will include applicable fees, if any, for the following use categories 
as defined in Administrative Regulation R-1411 pursuant to the provisions of the 
Civic Center Act.  (Education Code §82537 et seq.) 
1.2.1 Class I:  These uses are for general-use classrooms, meeting and lecture 
facilities, where the event takes place during normal business hours and 
the user is a public entity, nonprofit organization, senior citizen 
organization, club or association formed for cultural, general character 
building or welfare purposes.  
1.2.2 Class II: Semi-Commercial Use. These are uses: 
1.2.2.1 Of organizations identified under Class I, but that charge 
admission fees or solicit contributions that are not used for the 
benefit of the students of the District or charitable purposes; or  
1.2.2.2 Of organizations identified under Class I, but where the event takes 
place after normal business hours; or 
1.2.2.3 That are District categorical or specially funded program activities. 
1.2.3 Class III: Commercial Use. These are uses when the sponsor charges an 
admission fee and is not an organization identified under Class I.   
1.3 The Chancellor shall develop Administrative Regulations regarding this policy.
