1.0  Election or Appointment of Officers 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees shall hold an annual 
organizational meeting to elect a president and a vice president from its members and 
a secretary at the first Board of Trustees meeting in December. (Ed. Code §  72000.) 
1.1.1 No officer shall hold consecutive terms.  
1.1.2 The President of the Board of Trustees may appoint a parliamentarian. 
1.2 In a year in which a regular election for Board of Trustee members is conducted, the 
organizational meeting shall be held on a day within a 15-day period that commences 
with the date upon which the Board of Trustee member elected at that election takes 
office. (See Ed. Code, § 5000.)  The organizational meeting during non-election 
years shall be held within the same 15-day period. 
2.0  Duties of Officers 
2.1 President 
2.1.1 To preside at all meetings of the Board of Trustees. 
2.1.2 To appoint or provide for the election of all committees. 
2.1.3 To call special meetings as required. 
2.1.4 To perform such other duties as may be prescribed by law or by action of 
the Board of Trustees. 
2.2 Vice-President 
2.2.1 To preside over Board of Trustee meetings in the absence of the President. 
2.2.1.1 In the absence of the Vice-President, Board of Trustee members 
may elect a president pro tem. 
2.3 Secretary to the Board 
2.3.1 To notify members of the Board of Trustees of all regular, special and 
adjourned meetings. 
2.3.2 To attend all Board of Trustee meetings except when the Secretary's 
status, salary or performance is being discussed. 
2.3.3 To record the minutes of the meetings of the Board of Trustees and 
transmit a copy of the minutes of the previous meeting to each member of 
the Board of Trustees.
