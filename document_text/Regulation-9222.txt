COMPENSATION AND SCHEDULE Holidays R-9222 
Work Schedule 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Observance of Holidays Falling on Saturday or Sunday 
1.1 Holidays will be observed on the appointed day, with the following exceptions: 
1.1.1 When a holiday falls on Sunday, the following Monday shall be observed 
as the holiday. 
1.1.2 When a holiday falls on Saturday, the preceding Friday shall be observed 
as the holiday.
