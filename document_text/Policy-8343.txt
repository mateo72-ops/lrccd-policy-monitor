1.0 Authorization to Drive District Vehicles 
1.1 Los Rios Community College District vehicles shall be driven and operated in 
accordance with District Administrative Regulations. 
2.0 Mileage Allowance 
2.1 An employee may be authorized to use a privately owned vehicle in the conduct 
of authorized District business. When such use is authorized, the employee may 
be reimbursed for mileage in accordance with District Administrative 
Regulations. 
3.0 Rules and Regulations  
3.1 The Chancellor or designee will develop Administrative Regulations to 
implement this District Policy.
