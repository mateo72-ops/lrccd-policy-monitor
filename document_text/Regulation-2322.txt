1.0 Use by Students 
1.1 Only Los Rios Community College District students, employees, and approved 
volunteers are authorized to ride in District vehicles unless a waiver is approved 
by authorized administrative personnel. 
1.2 If a student goes on any College-sponsored trip in a District vehicle, the student 
must return in the District vehicle unless a prior waiver has been approved by 
authorized administrative personnel. 
1.3 Driving vehicles between 12:00 midnight and 6:00 a.m. is prohibited without 
prior approval of the Vice President of Administration. 
1.4 For off-campus club activities, the College advisor is expected to supervise the 
entire activity, to include traveling with the students to and from the activity 
destination.  An exception to this policy is for off-campus location(s) within a 
twenty-five (25) mile radius of the college sponsoring the activity and the 
District/College is not providing transportation.  (Personal vehicle(s) of the 
college advisor cannot be used to transport students.)
