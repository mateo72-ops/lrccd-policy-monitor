EMPLOYEE DISCIPLINE 
 
Severe Disciplinary Action P-6914 
Disciplinary Procedures 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Severe Disciplinary Action 
1.1 The Chancellor may impose severe discipline on an employee whenever the 
Chancellor determines that the employee: 
1) has violated Board Policy or other applicable regulations or laws and has not 
modified the behavior after having been apprized of the violation and given an 
opportunity to correct the inappropriate conduct; or  
2) whenever the employee has committed an act which is so severe as to warrant 
immediate action. If the discipline is not appealed, the Chancellor shall submit 
the discipline to the Board for final action. 
2.0 Employee Rights 
2.1 An employee shall not be the subject of severe discipline except for causes 
designated in Board policies and regulations, the Education Code, the Penal Code 
or other applicable law. 
2.2 An employee shall have the right to a hearing as outlined in Policy 6915. 
2.3 An employee who has abandoned a position shall not have a right to a hearing 
prior to termination.
