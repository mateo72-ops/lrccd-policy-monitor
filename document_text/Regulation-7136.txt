1.0 Determination of Undue Burden/Fundamental Alteration 
1.1 The determination of whether making ICT accessible will place an undue burden 
on the District/College, or fundamentally alter the nature of the educational 
program or service, shall be made in accordance with the procedures of Board 
Policy and Administrative Regulation 2731.  
 
1.2 Any determination that making the ICT accessible will place an undue burden on 
the District/College shall be made in consultation with the Vice President of 
Student Services and the General Counsel.
