Pay Periods and Procedures P-6326 
Compensation for Services 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Accounting for Time Worked 
1.1 Classified employees shall report work time and absences in accordance with the 
provisions of the administrative regulations established for the District 
2.0 Computing Compensation  
2.1 Regular employees with twelve month work assignments will be paid based on 
the appropriate monthly rate for the position classification 
2.2 All other employees will be paid at the appropriate hourly rate for the work 
assignments. 
3.0 Payroll Deductions 
3.1 Deductions from employees' pay will be those authorized by the Education Code, 
the State and Federal governments. 
4.0 Classified Teaching Opportunities 
4.1 Classified employees may be employed as adjunct faculty as authorized by 
regulations adopted by the Chancellor.
