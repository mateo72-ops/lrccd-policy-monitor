Problem Resolution 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Definition 
1.1 Problem - For the purpose of this policy, a problem shall be a charge by a 
management or confidential employee that the employee has been adversely 
affected by a misinterpretation, violation, or misapplication of written policies of 
the Board of Trustees, or written rules or regulations of the District. 
2.0 Problem Resolution Process 
2.1 Any employee having a problem may discuss the matter informally with the 
immediate supervisor in an effort to resolve the problem. 
2.2 If the problem is not resolved informally, a management or confidential employee 
may request an administrative review. 
2.3 Neither the Board nor any member of the administration shall take reprisals 
affecting the employment status of any management or confidential employee of 
the District by reason of the employee's participation in the administrative review 
procedure.
