DISTRICT CLASSIFICATION PLAN Reclassification of Individual Positions R-6222 
Maintenance of the Plan 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Criteria for Reclassification 
1.1 Reclassification of a position will be the result of a study which shows the 
following: 
1.1.1 The position when compared to other positions in the same class (same 
descriptive title) is now dissimilar and requires the incumbent to perform 
duties at a higher or lower level than the typical duties assigned to other 
positions in the current class;  
1.1.2 the position demands that the incumbent assume greater or lesser 
responsibility than is required in other positions assigned to the current 
class; 
1.1.3 the skills, education and experience needed for successfully performing 
the job exceed the requirements of other positions currently assigned to 
the same class or are less than the current requirements.
