Transfer of Credit P-2216 
Enrollment 1 of 1 
 
1.0  Acceptance of Transfer Credit 
1.1 Los Rios Colleges will accept credit for work completed in accredited institutions 
of post-secondary education as identified in recognized credit guides and 
publications. 
1.2 Work taken in non-accredited institutions may be considered for purposes of 
placement or as a basis for taking special examinations for credit. 
1.3 The Chancellor shall develop administrative rules and regulations to implement 
the intent of this policy.
