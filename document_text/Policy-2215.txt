1.0  Transcript of Records 
1.1 Upon request by a student, the Records/Admissions Office will send the student’s 
transcript of academic records from that Los Rios Community colleges to any 
college or university. (Education Code § 76225) 
1.2 The college making the transfer of records shall notify the student of the student’s 
right to receive a copy of the transcript, and the right to a hearing to challenge its 
contents.  
1.3 A student’s official college transcript from another college or university or from a 
high school shall not be released to the student or to another party without a court 
order.
