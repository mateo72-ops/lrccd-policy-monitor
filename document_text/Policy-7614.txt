1.0 Authorization 
1.1 The Chancellor/Superintendent is authorized by the Board of Trustees to establish 
the position of Department Chair Spokesperson/Coordinator to act as a liaison 
between department members and the College administration. 
2.0 Qualifications and Selection 
2.1 Spokesperson/Coordinator candidates, which must be individuals with 
employment rights, shall be chosen by a simple majority vote in department 
elections. A candidate so elected is recommended to the College President for 
appointment to a two-year term which commences with the fall semester 
following the election. If a recommendation is not accepted by the College 
President, another candidate will be selected by election and recommended. 
2.1.1 Elections shall be held in April of odd-numbered years. 
2.1.2 Each certificated member of the department with employment rights shall 
have one (1) vote. 
2.1.3 Incumbents may be reelected. 
2.2 An incumbent spokesperson/coordinator may be recalled. Petition of one-third of 
the department's eligible voters shall be required for a recall election. Removal 
from office shall require a two-thirds majority vote in such election. 
2.3 A special election shall be held within thirty (30) days of any vacancy. 
2.3.1 If the remaining term is one (1) semester or less (excluding summer) the 
replacement shall automatically serve the succeeding term. 
2.3.2 If the remaining term is more than one (1) semester (excluding summer) 
the special election shall apply only to the unexpired term. 
3.0 Definition of Department Status 
3.1 Each college shall determine its own department structure based on the following 
criteria, using the numerical and fiscal parameters assigned by administrative 
regulation: 
3.1.1 Number of members (certificated/classified; day/evening); 
3.1.2 complexity of subject areas; 
3.1.3 size of budget; 
3.1.4 geographic relationship to campus and outreach centers; 
3.1.5 communication needs;
