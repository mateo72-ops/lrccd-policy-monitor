BUILDING CONSTRUCTION PROGRAM 
 
Guidelines for Site Selection R-8411 
General Planning 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Guidelines for Site Selection 
1.1 Assessment of needs and preferences shall consider the following elements: 
1.1.1 Adequate identification of the community area and characteristics of 
individuals to be served. 
1.1.2 Projections of potential enrollment demand in the service area that 
demonstrate significant unmet need, taking into account plans of nearby 
secondary and postsecondary institutions. 
1.1.3 Evidence of significant community support and identification of possible 
community opposition. 
1.1.4 Identification of preferences for community college programs and services 
on the part of individuals in the service area. 
1.1.5 Identification, insofar as possible, of present and future labor market 
requirements for the proposed service area, a broader adjacent region, and 
the state. 
1.1.6 A reconciliation of educational services with projected labor market 
requirements and community program preferences, if possible. 
1.2 Identifications of Objectives 
1.2.1 Proposed programs, services and defined elements should be sufficiently 
specific so that the District governing Board may appropriately evaluate 
the options or selections. 
1.3 Coordination with the State Chancellor’s Office 
1.3.1 The District shall follow state approved capital outlay guidelines for the 
site acquisition if the site is planned for a college or state recognized 
educational center. 
1.3.2 The District is expected to work with the Chancellor’s Office to ensure 
that the location and development potential of the site is compatible with 
statewide development plan for higher education.
