1.0 Student Employment Services 
1.1 Each College of the Los Rios Community College District shall maintain a 
student Job Services program to assist in providing on- and off-campus 
employment support for currently enrolled students, Associate Degree graduates, 
and certificate earners, provided adequate funding is available.
