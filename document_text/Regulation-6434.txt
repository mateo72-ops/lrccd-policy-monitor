ABSENCES AND LEAVES  Personal Business R-6434 
Notification and Absence Reporting 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Procedure 
1.1 The employee will discuss with the supervisor the need to use time off for the 
care of personal business matters prior to being absent. Approval of the 
administrative officer for use of personal business time is required.
