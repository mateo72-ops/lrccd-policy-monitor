COMPENSATION AND RELATED BENEFITS 
 
Overtime R-6323 
Compensation for Services 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Overtime Pay for Temporary Employees 
1.1 Temporary employees will be compensated at time and one-half for all time 
worked over eight (8) hours a day and/or forty (40) hours in a workweek. At the 
discretion of the Chancellor/designee, certain temporary employees may be 
assigned an alternate schedule, as defined in the Education Code. (Ed. Code 
88027) 
2.0 Supervisory Responsibility for Authorization of Overtime 
2.1 Supervisors must authorize an employee to work overtime either in writing or 
with clear verbal directions given in advance of the overtime to be worked. The 
supervisor is to sign each employee's overtime time sheet
