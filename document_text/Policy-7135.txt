1.0  General Statement 
1.1 Articulation is the process of faculty review and evaluation used to determine in 
what manner course work completed at one institution will meet requirements for 
admissions, transfer credit, general education or major preparation at another 
institution.  
1.2 The intent of this Los Rios Community College District Policy and the 
accompanying Administrative Regulations is to ease the process of moving 
students from high school to the District Colleges and from the District Colleges 
to four year institutions. The District shall endeavor to eliminate barriers and 
simplify the process wherever possible. 
1.3 Appropriate college content area faculty at each College shall have the primary 
responsibility for facilitating the development of articulation agreements with 
their feeder high schools. Articulation Officers at each College shall have primary 
responsibility for facilitating the maintenance of high school articulation 
agreements.  
1.4 Articulation Officers at each College shall have the primary responsibility for 
facilitating the development and maintenance of articulation agreements with four 
year institutions of higher education.  
1.5 Courses and programs, as deemed appropriate at each college, shall be articulated 
with proximate high schools. 
1.6 The Chancellor shall adopt regulations to provide for implementation of this 
policy. 
1.7 Students may receive college credit for articulated high school courses.
