ADMISSIONS AND RECORDS 
 
Notice and Pause P-2231 
Academic Standards 1 of 2 
 
 
1.0  Eligibility for Good Standing 
1.1 For purposes of determining a student’s eligibility to remain in good standing and 
in attendance at one of the Colleges of the Los Rios Community College District, 
both quality of performance and progress toward completion of objectives shall 
be considered. 
2.0  Academic and Progress Notice  
2.1 Each College within the District shall implement the academic and progress 
notification intervention practices consistent with Title 5, section 55031 and R-2231. 
 
2.2 Academic notice shall be based on grade point average. 
 
2.3 Progress notice shall be based on percentage of earned non-evaluative symbols and 
entries of “NP” (formerly “NC”) as described in R-7252. 
 
2.4 Academic records accrued during summer and/or intersessions shall count toward 
notice, removal from notice, or possible pause. 
 
3.0  Academic and Progress Pause 
3.1 A student shall be subject to a pause intervention from college under certain 
circumstances related to continuing notice as stipulated in Title 5, section 55033 and 
R-2231. 
 
3.2 A student may petition for re-admission following placement on pause. 
 
4.0 Eligibility for California College Promise Grant and Priority 
Registration 
4.1 A student who is otherwise eligible for the California College Promise Grant (Board 
of Governor’s Fee Waiver) shall become ineligible if the student is placed on 
academic or progress notice or any combination thereof for two (2) consecutive 
primary terms. (Title 5, § 58621, subd. (a)(1)) 
 
4.2 A student who is otherwise eligible for Priority Registration pursuant to R-2211 shall 
become ineligible if the student is placed on academic or progress notice, or any 
combination thereof, for two (2) consecutive primary terms. (Title 5, § 58108)
