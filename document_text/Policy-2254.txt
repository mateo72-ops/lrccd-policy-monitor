Student Fees 1 of 1 
 
1.0  Student Enrollment Fee 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees shall charge each 
student a fee which is established by the State of California for enrolling in credit 
courses pursuant to the requirements of Education Code section  76300 and 
Education Code section 76330 and California Code of Regulations, title 5 
section  58500. 
2.0  Deferments or Installment Payment of Fees 
2.1 Payment of fees may be deferred as prescribed in Los Rios Community College 
District Administrative Regulations. 
3.0  Refund of Fees 
3.1 Refunds to students for general enrollment fees or differential fees shall follow 
guidelines established by the state.
