EMPLOYMENT PROCEDURES 
 
Promotion P-6134 
Assignment and Reassignment   1 of 1 
 
1.0 Intent 
1.1 It is the District's desire that capable and energetic employees have the 
opportunity to be promoted to positions of greater responsibility and salary. 
2.0 Criterion for Promotion 
2.1 A promotion shall require that the overall fitness of the candidate be determined 
by evaluation, verification of skills, and/or testing procedures such as are required 
for initial appointment. 
3.0 Selection 
3.1 Selection shall be made on the basis of individual qualifications and capabilities 
of the candidate, and in the best interests and needs of the District.
