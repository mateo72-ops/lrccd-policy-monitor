1.0 Membership in PERS 
1.1 Regular employees (working full time or fifty percent (50%) or more part time) 
become members of PERS at time of hire. 
1.2 Regular employees working less than fifty percent (50%) of full time are excluded 
from membership unless they are already members. (Gov. Code 20334) 
1.3 Short-term and substitute employees are excluded from membership unless they 
are already members, or employment will be for full time, more than six (6) 
months. (Gov. Code 20336)
