1.0 Student Choice of College 
1.1 Students may attend any college of the District and participate in inter-college 
athletic programs provided the student adheres to the academic standards of the 
District and the Athletic Code of the Commission on Athletics of the Community 
College League of California (CCLC). 
2.0 Active Recruitment 
2.1 Active recruitment means the following: 
2.1.1 Visits to high schools; 
2.1.2 any contact initiated by a coach with the intent of encouraging a student to 
attend a particular college. 
2.2 The colleges will develop a promotional program to encourage increased 
participation of women in the college's athletic programs. 
3.0 Official Classes 
3.1 The Los Rios Community College District considers the fall intercollegiate sports 
to be official classes even though practice begins prior to the opening date of 
other college classes. A student entering these classes at any college in the District 
shall be considered officially enrolled in that college as of the first date of his/her 
participation in practice. 
4.0 Student Transfer 
4.1 Transfers between and among the colleges of the District shall be subject to the 
sections of the Athletic Code which governs transfers. 
5.0 Dissemination of Information 
5.1 High schools of the District will be notified of the rules and regulations governing 
athletic participation by the Los Rios District. 
6.0 Los Rios District Athletic Committee 
6.1 A Los Rios District Athletic Committee shall be formed consisting of the athletic 
directors, the designated representative to the athletic Conference, and the Title 
IX coordinators of each college, or other designee, as determined by the College 
President and a liaison officer representing the Chancellor. 
6.2 This committee shall meet at least once each semester. The chair shall rotate 
among the colleges. An athletic director and a designated representative shall 
alternately serve as chairperson.
