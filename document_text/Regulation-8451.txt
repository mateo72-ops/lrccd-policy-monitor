1.0 Construction Sites – Construction 
1.1 Unauthorized personnel shall not be permitted on any construction site on District 
property unless approved pursuant to this regulation. 
1.2 Visits and/or tours of construction sites may be permitted as follows: 
1.2.1 Any person or persons desiring to enter upon a construction site shall 
submit a request to the Vice President of Administration or designee. The 
request will state the purpose of the visit, the desired date, time and 
duration, the number of visitors and any other relevant information. 
1.2.2 The Vice President of Administration or designee shall notify the District's 
Project Manager to determine whether the visit is appropriate and safe. 
1.2.3 The Vice President of Administration or designee will advise the 
requesting party if, and/or, when the entry will be permitted and will 
advise of any conditions related to such entry.
