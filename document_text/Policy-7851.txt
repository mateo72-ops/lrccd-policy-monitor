Privacy and Confidentiality   1 of 1 
 
1.0 Privacy 
1.1 The Systems are Los Rios Community College District property provided for 
authorized use described in the District policies, regulations, and guidelines and 
are subject to monitoring at any time with or without notice, at management's sole 
discretion. 
1.2 Absolute privacy of user data cannot be guaranteed for a number of technical, 
legal and economic reasons. Among them are limitations in software and 
hardware technology, limited personnel and financial resources, and human error. 
The District cannot, given these limitations, guarantee the privacy of user data. 
1.3 This policy does not limit the meaning or scope of state law provisions regarding 
confidentiality of employee records.
