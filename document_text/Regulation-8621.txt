CONFLICT OF INTEREST CODE 
 
Complimentary Tickets   R-8621 
Complimentary Tickets 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Administrator 
1.1 The Deputy Chancellor (or Vice Chancellor, Finance and Administration, if there 
is no Deputy Chancellor) or designee shall be the ticket administrator responsible 
for implementing Complimentary Ticket Policy P-8621. 
1.2 The ticket administrator shall be responsible for reporting the distribution of 
tickets required below. 
1.3 The ticket administrator will establish procedures for the distribution of tickets in 
accordance with Complimentary Ticket Policy P-8621. At a minimum, the ticket 
administrator will: 
1.3.1 Manage the receipt, distribution, and accounting for all tickets or passes 
subject to Complimentary Ticket Policy P-8621. 
1.3.2 Ensure tickets or passes distributed by the Los Rios Community College 
District/College under this policy serve at least one of the public purposes 
as defined in Complimentary Ticket Policy P-8621. 
1.3.3 Ensure tickets distributed pursuant to this Complimentary Ticket Policy P-
8621 are disclosed on the Fair Political Practices Commission “FPPC” 
Form.  
1.3.4 Each disclosure form shall include: 
1.3.4.1 The name of the person receiving the ticket or pass. 
1.3.4.2 A description of the event. 
1.3.4.3 The date of the event. 
1.3.4.4 The face value of the ticket or pass. 
1.3.4.5 The number of tickets or passes provided to each person. 
1.3.4.6 If the ticket or pass was behested, the name of the official who 
behested the ticket.  
1.3.4.7 A description of the public purpose under which the distribution 
was made, or alternatively, that the ticket or pass was distributed as 
income to the official. 
1.3.5 Sign each disclosure form verifying that the distribution of tickets is in 
accordance with the provisions of FPPC Regulation section 18944.1. 
1.3.6 Ensure the disclosure form is provided to the FPPC in a timely fashion.
