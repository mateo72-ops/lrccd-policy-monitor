CLASSIFICATION PLAN 
 
Adoption of Plan and Allocation to 
Classes   
P-6211 
Authorization for Plan 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Adoption of Plan 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees shall adopt and 
maintain a classification plan showing the relative salary ranges for all positions 
in the classified service. (Ed. Code, §§ 88003; 88004; 88009.) 
2.0 Specifications for Position Classes 
2.1 Specifications for each position class shall be established by Human Resources 
and approved by the Chancellor or designee. 
3.0 Position Allocation 
3.1 Upon the recommendation of the Associate Vice Chancellor, Human Resources, 
the Chancellor or designee shall allocate every position in the classified service to 
one of the classes established in the classification plan. (Ed. Code, § 88004)
