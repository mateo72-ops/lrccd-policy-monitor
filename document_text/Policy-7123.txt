INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAM 
 
Academic Calendar P-7123 
Instructional Program 1 of 1 
 
1.0  Authorization 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Chancellor is responsible for the 
development and preparation of the calendar for the academic year’s operation. 
1.2 The Chancellor will submit the calendar to the Board in sufficient time to allow 
representatives of management and of the bargaining unit to meet on the calendar 
according to the labor contract before January 15.
