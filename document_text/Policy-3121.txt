ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEDURES 
 
Trustee Elections   P-3121 
Election Procedures 1 of 1 
 
1.0  Candidates’ Statements in Trustee Elections 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District shall not pay the cost of candidates' 
statements or other materials sent to voters of the District.   (Elect. Code, § 13307) 
1.2 This policy shall be delivered to the appropriate elections officer in ample time for 
such officer to notify each candidate of the Los Rios Community College District 
Board of Trustees’ position before the nominating period opens. 
1.3 The Board of Trustees authorizes the Chancellor to estimate the total cost of printing, 
handling, translating and mailing of candidate's statements and to require each 
candidate filing a statement to pay in advance to the District a pro rata share as a 
condition to having each candidate's statement included in the voter's pamphlet. 
2.0  Selection in Case of Tie Vote (Ed. Code, § 5016.) 
2.1 If a tie vote makes it impossible to determine which of two or more candidates has 
been elected to the Board of Trustees, the county superintendent of schools having 
jurisdiction shall so certify to the Board of Trustees. 
2.2 The Board of Trustees shall make a determination of who is the winner by lot. 
2.3 The candidates who have received the tie votes shall be notified to appear before the 
Board of Trustees, either in person or by a representative at a time and place 
designated by the Board of Trustees, and the determination by lot shall be made at 
that time.
