1.0  Clubs and Organizations 
1.1 Impetus for formation of a new club on the campus is expected to come from the 
students. 
1.2 All clubs are required to abide by legal provisions governing such clubs, 
applicable regulations of the Board of Trustees, and campus regulations. 
(Education Code § 76120; Cross Reference R-2411, Sections 1.3 and 1.4) 
1.3 Every club must have a faculty advisor in order to operate on the campus. 
(Cross Reference R-2411)
