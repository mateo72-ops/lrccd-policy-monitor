1.0 Academic Standards Criteria 
1.1 Academic Notice 
A student of the who has received transcript notation in at least twelve (12) 
semester units at a Los Rios college and has earned a grade point average below 
2.0 at that college shall be placed on academic notice. (Title 5, § 55031, subd. (a), 
R-7252(1.1)) 
1.2 Progress Notice 
A student who has received transcript notation in at least twelve (12) semester 
units at a Los Rios college, and for whom at least 50% of the units at that college 
are recorded as “W”, “I”, “NP” and/or “NC” on the student’s transcript, shall be 
placed on progress notice. (Title 5, § 55031, subd. (b), R-7251(1.2-1.3)) 
1.3 Academic Pause 
A student on academic notice at a Los Rios college who earns a cumulative grade 
point average of less than 2.0 in all units attempted in each of the three (3) 
consecutive semesters shall be subject to academic pause. (Title 5, § 55033, R-
7252(1.1)) 
1.4 Progress Pause 
A student on progress notice at a Los Rios college, and for whom at least 50% of 
the units at the college in at least three (3) consecutive semesters are recorded as 
“W”, “I”, “NP” and/or “NC” on the student’s transcript shall be placed on 
progress pause. (Title 5, § 55033, R-7252(1.2-1.3)) 
1.5 Terms of Enrollment 
Enrollment includes fall and spring semesters (primary terms), summer sessions, 
or intersessions. For purposes of academic and progress pause determination, 
semesters shall be considered consecutive based on the student’s enrollment, so 
long as the break in the student’s enrollment does not exceed one full primary 
term. (Title 5, § 55033)

4.0 Academic and Progress Pause Interventions 
4.1 Students who are placed on academic or progress pause shall receive transcript

6.0  California College Promise Grant Eligibility 
6.1 Each College within the District shall inform students by prominently displaying and 
disseminating information about California College Promise Grant (CCPG) 
eligibility and student support services available to assist them in maintaining and 
reestablishing CCPG eligibility. Dissemination includes, but is not limited to, 
information provided in college catalogs and class schedules. (Title 5, § 58621, subd. 
(b)) 
6.2 Students placed on academic or progress notice shall be notified of their status no 
later than thirty (30) days following the end of the term that resulted in the student 
being placed on academic or progress notice. The notice shall state that two (2) 
consecutive primary terms of notice will lead to loss of the (CCPG) until the student 
is no longer on notice. This notice shall also advise students about the available 
student support services to assist them in maintaining eligibility. (Title 5, § 58621, 
subd. (d)(1)) 
6.3 The District shall establish written procedures by which a student may appeal the 
loss of the CCPG due to extenuating circumstances, or where a student with a 
disability applied for, but did not receive, reasonable accommodation in a timely 
manner. (Title 5, § 58621, subd. (e)) 
6.3.1 Extenuating circumstances are verified cases of accidents, illnesses, or 
other circumstances that might include documented changes in the 
student’s economic situation or evidence that the student was unable to 
obtain essential student support services. Essential student support 
services include, but are not limited to, services identified in the student 
education plan developed under Title 5, section 55524, and any services to 
which the student was referred pursuant to Title 5, section 55520. 
Extenuating circumstances also includes special consideration of specific 
factors associated with Veterans, CalWORKs, EOPS, and DSPS student 
status. (Title 5, § 58621, subd. (e))  
6.4 Students who have demonstrated significant academic improvement may retain or 
appeal the loss of the CCPG. Significant academic improvement is defined as 
achieving no less than the minimum grade point average and progress standard 
established in Title 5, section 55031(a). (Title 5, § 58621, subd. (e))
