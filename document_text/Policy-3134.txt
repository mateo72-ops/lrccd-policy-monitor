ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEDURES

Board Organization 1 of 1 
 
1.0  Committees of the Los Rios Community College District Board of 
Trustees 
1.1 It shall be the policy of the Board of Trustees not to operate by subcommittees, but 
where necessary and when authorized by Board of Trustee action, the President may 
appoint subcommittees and the chairperson thereof. 
1.2 The Chancellor shall be notified of the time and place of all committee meetings and 
shall be eligible to attend all such meetings, except when the Chancellor's 
employment status, evaluation or salary is under consideration. 
1.3 A Board of Trustees' committee shall meet at the call of the chairperson of the 
committee. 
1.4 The Secretary to the Board of Trustees or the chairperson of the committee shall give 
advance notice to all committee members and other eligible attendees of the time and 
place of meetings. 
2.0  Trustees Representatives 
2.1 The Board of Trustees shall appoint the Trustee representatives to organizations in 
which the Los Rios Community College District has membership. 
3.0  Institutional Membership 
3.1 A list of all organizations for which the District has institutional memberships shall 
be maintained.
