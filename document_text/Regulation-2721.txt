Ancillary Services 1 of 3 
 
1.0 Children's Center Facility  
1.1 There may be developed at each college upon recommendation of the College 
President and approval of the Chancellor/Superintendent, a children's center that 
is designed to accommodate the number of eligible children as determined by a 
survey conducted by college personnel. 
1.2 In order to be able to provide a full schedule of programs at a feasible cost, the 
initial installation at each site will accommodate not less than 30 children per 
hour. 
1.3 Campus children's centers may be operated in facilities provided for this purpose 
which are located on or near the College campus and which meet facility 
requirements of the approving agency. 
2.0 Eligibility 
2.1 Age of eligible participants. (Educ. Code 79122) 
2.1.1 Children from six (6) months to school age are eligible. 
2.2 Admission priority 
2.2.1 Children of students of the college of attendance by the parent shall have 
first priority. (Educ. Code 79123) 
2.2.1.1 Priority rankings within this group are determined by family 
income and single-parent or two-parent status in accordance with 
regulations established by law. 
3.0 Financial Support  
3.1 Federal and State Subventions 
3.1.1 The children's centers shall be managed and reported in such a manner as 
to maximize Federal and State income. 
3.2 Participant fees 
3.2.1 Fees charged participants shall be in accordance with a fee schedule 
established by the Superintendent of Public Instruction. (Educ. Code 
8263(e)) 
3.2.2 No fee shall be charged to any recipient of public assistance.

4.0 Program Costs  
4.1 Program costs shall mean all program costs that are related to the operation of 
children’s center programs, including the State and District contribution to 
retirement benefits, pro-rata share of District's overhead, and any other program-
related costs. (Educ. Code 8208(n)) 
4.2 Program costs shall be limited to approved non-operating expense purposes plus 
that amount per child hour that may be obtained in reimbursement from the 
Federal and State sources except that each center shall receive minimum 
operating support funds to maintain the center, based on the workday, workyear, 
and number of children served as approved by State regulations. (Educ. Code 
8265, Educ. Code 8265.5) 
5.0 Program 
5.1 The children's center shall develop and promote a full range of services which 
shall include the following as a minimum: 
5.1.1 Developmental activities designed to prepare the child for entrance into a 
formal school program; 
5.1.2 full- or part-time supervision, developmental activities and instruction for 
children from six (6) months to school age in an approved program; 
5.1.3 instruction in children's developmental activities for parents with 
prekindergarten children; 
5.1.4 social services as necessary to insure parent-child adjustments to out-of-
home-care situations, and other support services when the parent is in 
training; 
5.1.5 health screening and health treatment as provided by Medi-Cal; 
5.1.6 nutrition services to provide a balanced diet to enrolled children. 
5.2 The children's center program is operated for the primary purpose of providing 
services outlined in 5.1 above, although such center may provide laboratory and 
observation experiences for students enrolled in a college instructional program. 
5.3 The children's center program shall maintain a ratio of two (2) teachers plus one 
(1) adult to fifteen (15) children participants per operational hour. Parent 
participation is encouraged where appropriate.

6.0 Personnel 
6.1 Rights and Privileges 
6.1.1 Employees of the District in the child development program have the same 
rights and privileges of other employees of the District. 
6.2 Salary Levels  
6.2.1 Personnel employed to perform duties in campus children's center 
programs are employed at salary levels commensurate with positions of 
comparable responsibility in other children's centers in the Sacramento 
area. 
6.3 Certification Qualifications (Educ. Code 8360) 
6.3.1 Certificated employees must hold regular child care and development 
permits issued by the Commission for Teacher Preparation and Licensing. 
6.3.2 Any person holding a credential authorizing teaching service in 
elementary schools issued by the State Board of Education or Commission 
with either twelve (12) units of training or at least two (2) years' 
experience in early childhood education or a child care and development 
program, or holding a single subject credential in home economics with 
either twelve (12) units of training or at least two (2) years' experience in 
early childhood education or a child care and development program is 
deemed to hold a regular child care and development permit that will 
authorize supervision and instruction of children or supervision of a child 
development program.
