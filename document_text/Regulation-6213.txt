DISTRICT CLASSIFICATION PLAN Determination of Service Categories R-6213 
Authorization for a Plan 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Assignment of Position Classes to Service Categories 
1.1 Classes of positions shall be assigned to one of the eleven service categories: 
1.1.1 Accounting/Budget/Purchasing 
1.1.2 Bookstore 
1.1.3 Campus Children's Center 
1.1.4 Construction Inspection 
1.1.5 Data Processing 
1.1.6 Food Services 
1.1.7 Maintenance and Operations 
1.1.8 Secretarial/Clerical 
1.1.9 Security  
1.1.10 Supervisory 
1.1.11 Technical/Instructional Support/Student Personnel Services 
1.2 Positions which cannot be a part of the classified services shall be assigned to 
Special Programs (short-term and student positions).
