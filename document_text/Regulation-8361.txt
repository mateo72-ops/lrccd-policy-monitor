1.0 Minimum Documentation Requirements for Payment of 
Expenditures for Approved Special Activities: 
1.1 Minimum documentation requirements for expenditures incurred for educational 
events to promote community awareness ((P-8361), Section 2.2.1) are: 
1.1.1 Purpose of the expense; 
1.1.2 Invitation or program; and 
1.1.3 Name(s) of attendee(s), or group name, or approximate number of 
attendees or invitees. 
1.2 Minimum documentation requirements of expenditures incurred for educational 
and planning seminars, workshops and meetings for employees and/or board ((P-
8361), Section 2.2.2) are:  
1.2.1 Name, title, description or purpose of the meeting; 
1.2.2 Announcement or agenda; and 
1.2.3 Names of attendees or invitees. If several persons of the same group are 
attending, terms such as "management team" or "Cabinet" may be used. 
The number of attendees should be specified if persons are not identified 
by name. 
1.3 Minimum documentation requirements for expenditures incurred by the 
designated official representative(s) ((P-8361), Section 2.2.3) shall be: 
1.3.1 Invitation, announcement or program, if available; and 
1.3.2 Name of designated representative(s). 
1.4 Minimum documentation requirements for expenditures incurred for functions for 
volunteers; new and retiring employees ((P-8361), Section 2.2.4) are: 
1.4.1 Purpose of the expense; 
1.4.2 Invitation or program; and 
1.4.3 Name(s) of honoree(s), or group name, or approximate number of 
attendees/participants. 
1.5 Minimum documentation requirements for activities which improve employee 
morale or express appreciation as defined in (P-8361), Section 2.2.5 shall be: 
1.5.1 The purpose of the expenditure, (name of recipient).
