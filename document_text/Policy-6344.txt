Insurance Programs 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Authorization 
1.1 The District participates in the state-wide program of unemployment insurance for 
regular classified employees. Eligibility of employees to receive benefits during 
periods of layoff or upon termination of employment is determined by the State 
Employment Development Department.
