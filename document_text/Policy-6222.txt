CLASSIFICATION PLAN 
 
Reclassification of Individual Position P-6222 
Maintenance of the Plan 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Authorization to Reclassify 
1.1 The Board of Trustees may, upon the recommendation of the 
Chancellor/Superintendent or designee, reclassify a position to a more appropriate 
class.  Reclassification of a position will be the result of a study which shows that 
the responsibility level and/or job duties of a particular position have changed 
sufficiently to justify reassignment of the position to another position class. 
1.2 Upon the recommendation of the Chancellor/Superintendent or designee, the 
Governing Board may reclassify position classes and individual positions.  
Reclassification normally becomes effective July 1 of a given year, although a 
different effective date may be used at the recommendation of the 
Chancellor/Superintendent or designee.  Position classes and individual positions 
will be allocated to the appropriate salary ranges approved by the Governing Board.
