SECURITY SERVICES 
 
General Conditions P-6711 
Los Rios Police Department 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Authority 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees hereby establishes a 
Los Rios Community College District Police Department in accordance with 
provisions of Education Code Section 72330. 
2.0 Supervision 
2.1 The Chief of Police, appointed in compliance with Education Code Section 
72330, shall supervise personnel assigned to the Los Rios Community College 
District Police Department.  
3.0 Regulations 
3.1 The Chancellor shall establish corresponding administrative regulations 
governing employees designated as Los Rios police officers.  These regulations 
shall include the use of handguns and other weapons.
