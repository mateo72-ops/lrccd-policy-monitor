EMPLOYMENT PROCEDURES 
 
Initial Assignment and Probation P-6131 
Assignment and Reassignment   1 of 1 
 
1.0 Probationary Status 
1.1 Each employee appointed to a regular position shall serve a probationary period 
of one (1) year. (Education Code § 88013) 
1.1.1 At designated times during the probationary period, the performance of 
the employee shall be reviewed by those having the responsibility for 
recommending permanent status. 
2.0 Permanent Status 
2.1 Upon satisfactory completion of the required probationary period, a regular 
classified employee shall achieve permanent status with the District. (See P-6151) 
3.0 Status -- Unclassified Positions 
3.1 Time spent in substitute, long-term temporary, short-term temporary, special 
services or student help positions does not count toward a probationary period in a 
regular position.
