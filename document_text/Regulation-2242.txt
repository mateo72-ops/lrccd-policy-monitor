1.0  Resident Student Defined 
1.1 Unless otherwise mandated by law, a resident student for purposes of admission 
as a student to the Colleges of Los Rios Community College District, means any 
person who has been a bona fide resident of the state for more than one (1) year 
immediately preceding the opening day of a semester or session during which the 
student proposed to attend a College of the District. (Ed. Code, § 68017) 
1.2 The Colleges shall evaluate exceptions to the residency requirements as provided 
in the Education Code and/or Title 5 Regulations. (Ed. Code, §§ 68000-68100, 
68122, 76140.5; Title 5, §§ 54000-54072) 
2.0 Authority to Determine Residence 
2.1 The President of each College is authorized to evaluate information presented by 
an applicant for admission and to make determinations of residence.
