1.0 Holidays (Education Code §§ 79020, 88203 and 88205.5) 
1.1 The following legal holidays will be observed: 
Independence Day 
Labor Day 
Admission Day (or an in-lieu day, should classes be held on Admission Day) 
Veterans’ Day 
Thanksgiving Day 
Christmas Day 
New Year’s Day 
Martin Luther King Jr.’s Birthday, third Monday in January 
Lincoln’s Birthday 
Washington’s Birthday (3rd Monday in February) 
Memorial Day 
1.2 The following Board granted holidays will be observed: The Friday after 
Thanksgiving; in addition to Christmas Day and New Year's Day, one-half the 
working days falling within the winter recess period; and the Friday before Easter. 
(Education Code § 79022) 
2.0 Eligibility for Holiday Pay (Education Code § 88203) 
2.1 For payroll purposes, confidential employees receive holiday pay for holidays that 
fall within their normal work year, provided the employees are in pay status for 
the working day before or after the holiday.
