1.0 Drug and Alcohol Testing of Drivers Holding Commercial Driver's 
Licenses 
1.1 The Board of Trustees, commits to the adoption, implementation, and 
enforcement of Omnibus Transportation Employees Testing Act of 1991, which 
requires the District to establish a drug and alcohol testing program for all 
employees who perform services requiring the possession of a commercial 
driver's license, specifically, busdriver/maintenance helpers, transportation 
supervisors, ground maintenance technicians and equipment mechanics II 
(collectively, covered employees). 
1.2 Pre-employment drug testing shall be required for covered employees.  Post-
accident, random, reasonable suspicion and return to duty/follow-up drug and 
alcohol testing shall be required for covered employees.  
1.3 The Chancellor shall develop administrative regulations which adhere to the U.S. 
Department of Transportation rules and regulations for drug and alcohol testing. 
1.4 Violation of this policy or related regulations will be grounds for employee 
discipline.
