1.0  Authorization to Extend Class Schedules 
1.1 It is the belief of the Board of Trustees that the interest of all students may best be 
served by offering classes where the demand exists. 
1.2 The Chancellor is authorized to extend day instruction, evening instruction and 
summer school instruction beyond the scheduled classes where the demand exists.
