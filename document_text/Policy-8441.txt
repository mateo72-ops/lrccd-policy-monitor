Facility Construction Process P-8451 
Facility of Construction 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Construction Sites- Observation 
1.1 Unauthorized personnel shall not be permitted on construction sites on district 
property. 
1.2 The Chancellor may establish regulations to permit entry on a limited basis to 
employees and/or the general public for the purpose of observing the progress of 
the construction.
