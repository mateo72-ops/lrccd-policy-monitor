EMPLOYMENT PROCEDURES 
 
Equivalency to Minimum Qualifications 
 
P-5123 
Recruitment and Appointment 1 of 1 
 
1.0  Philosophy  
1.1 It is the policy of the Los Rios Community College District that faculty hiring 
procedures and guidelines be established to provide for highly qualified faculty -
experts in their subject matter areas, skilled in teaching and serving the needs of a 
varied student population, who can foster overall college effectiveness and are 
sensitive to and themselves represent the racial and cultural diversity of the District 
community. 
1.2 The Chancellor shall establish regulations for determining equivalencies to minimum 
qualifications.
