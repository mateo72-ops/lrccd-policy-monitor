ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEDURES 
 
Public Access to Information   P-3141 
Public Access 1 of 1 
 
1.0  General 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees recognizes the right of 
the public to information concerning all of its actions and policies and concerning the 
details of its educational and business operations. 
1.2 Information concerning the policies and educational programs of the Los Rios 
Community College District and the Colleges shall be disseminated throughout the 
District and the community. 
1.3 Members of the public may inspect all public records, as defined by the Government 
Code, during the regular office hours of the District. 
1.4 Copies of the policies and regulations shall be on file in the office of the Chancellor 
of the District, in the offices of the College Presidents and available in the College 
libraries. The official form of the policies and regulations of the District shall be 
maintained on line.
