INSTRUCTIONAL COMPUTER USE 
 
Access P-7821 
Implementation 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Access 
1.1 No person shall have access to the Los Rios Community College District Systems 
unless authorized by a District Administrator in the manner set forth in these 
Policies and Administrative Regulations. 
1.2 Users should be provided with access to a copy of these Policies and 
Administrative Regulations. 
1.3 Designated administrators may develop additional procedures applicable for use 
of the applicable computer system.
