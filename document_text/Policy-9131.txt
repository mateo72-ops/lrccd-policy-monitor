EMPLOYMENT PROCEDURES 
 
Transfer P-9131 
Change in Position or Location 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Definition 
1.1 Transfer is a change from one unit to another unit in the same position or from 
one position to another position. 
1.2 Unit is defined as a college or the District Office. 
2.0 Purpose  
2.1 The transfer of management or confidential employees shall be in the best interest 
of the District. 
2.1.1 Employer-initiated transfers must be based on one of the following 
reasons: Abolishment of a management position; over-staffing; need for 
special preparation in a specific management area; need for improvement 
in staff relationships or management operations as determined by the 
Chancellor. 
3.0 Authority 
3.1 The Chancellor shall inform the Board prior to approving transfers. 
 (Formerly R-9121)
