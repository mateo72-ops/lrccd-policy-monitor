1.0 Demotion Procedures 
1.1 The Chancellor may recommend to the Board that a management employee be 
demoted to a management position of lower classification without recruiting 
further for the vacant position. 
1.2 Management employees not in agreement with such a recommendation may 
implement procedures outlined in Administrative Regulation, (R-9321). 
1.3 Demotion shall be considered disciplinary action and shall be subject to policies 
and regulations concerning employee discipline.
