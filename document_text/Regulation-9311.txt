Management Confidential Relations 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Consultation with Management and Confidential Employees 
1.1 Meetings and conferences will be held with the management and confidential 
employees on issues of interest to those employees. 
1.2 The improvement of employer-employee relations will be promoted by providing 
open channels of communication for the expression of management and 
confidential employees.
