STUDENT DEVELOPMENT 
 
Recruitment Procedures P-2331 
Intercollegiate Athletics 1 of 1 
 
1.0   Eligibility 
1.1 Any student is eligible for participation in the intercollegiate athletic program at a 
Los Rios college if academic and state athletic code requirements are met. 
2.0 Recruitment 
2.1 Active recruitment throughout the District is allowed. 
2.2 Athletic Program Equity 
2.2.1 The colleges will organize their athletic programs in a manner that ensures 
that no person will be excluded from participation, denied benefits, treated 
differently, or in any way be discriminated against on the basis of gender 
in the program's activities.
