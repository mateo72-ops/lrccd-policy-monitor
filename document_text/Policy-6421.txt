ABSENCES AND LEAVES 
 
Sick Leave P-6421 
Paid Health Absences/Leaves 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Unused Sick Leave 
1.1 Unused portions of earned sick leave shall be cumulative on a year-to-year basis 
indefinitely. Unused cumulative sick leave will be cancelled when employment 
with the District is terminated and will not be compensated for in terminal pay. 
1.2 Whenever a former employee is reemployed within a twelve-month period 
following termination of former services, unused earned sick leave from the prior 
period of employment shall be reinstated.
