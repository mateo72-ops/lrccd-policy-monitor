1.0 Procedure for Filing a Resignation Notice  
1.1 The employee may obtain a notice of resignation form from the administrative 
officer or the Personnel Services Office. 
1.2 The notice of resignation shall be forwarded to the Personnel Services Office 
immediately. 
1.3 The employee will need to arrange for an exit interview with a personnel staff 
member and an administrative services staff member so that the individual can 
complete the necessary forms and receive an explanation of accrued benefits (if 
any). The employee may complete a termination questionnaire at the time of the 
exit interview.
