1.0 Equal Opportunity Employment on Construction Projects 
1.1 In view of the scope of activities involved on construction projects, the Board of 
Trustees recognizes that opportunities for employment of small emerging 
businesses are created when contracts are let for products and services. 
1.2 The Chancellor shall adopt regulations to ensure that small emerging businesses 
receive notice and have the opportunity to bid on District projects.
