P-6625 
Permanent Separation 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Termination of Employment 
1.1 A probationary employee may be terminated by the Governing Board at any time 
with or without cause upon the recommendation of the Chancellor. 
1.2 A conditional employee may be returned to a previously held position at any time 
upon the recommendation of the Chancellor. 
2.0 No Right of Appeal 
2.1 No appeal is available to probationary or conditional employees.
