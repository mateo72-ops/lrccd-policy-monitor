1.0  Trustee Areas 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees consists of seven (7) 
members elected from trustee areas by electors living in trustee area boundaries.  
Elections are held in accordance with the provisions of the California Education and 
Elections codes in November of even numbered years.  (Ed. Code, § 5000 and 5300, 
et seq.) 
1.2 Following each decennial federal census, the Board of Trustees shall adjust the 
boundaries of any or all of the trustee areas of the Los Rios Community College 
District in accordance with the requirements of Education Code, section 5019.5. 
2.0  Terms of Office 
2.1 Terms of office are four (4) years on a staggered basis.  (Ed. Code, § 5017, 72022) 
2.2 Trustees for areas one, two and six are elected in 1990 and every four (4) years 
thereafter.  Trustees for areas three, four, five and seven are elected in 1992 and 
every four years thereafter.
