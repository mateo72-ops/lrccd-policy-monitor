1.0 Authorization 
1.1 Provisions for a forty-hour workweek do not restrict the supervisor or 
administrative officer from extending the regular work schedule to an overtime 
basis when such is necessary to carry on the business of the District.  (Appendices 
B, C, & D) 
1.2 For all non-exempt employees, work performed in excess of eight (8) hours in any 
one day and/or in excess of forty (40) hours in a workweek, as defined in each 
collective bargaining agreement, is considered overtime.  (Educ. Code § 88027)
