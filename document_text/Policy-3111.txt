1.0  Authority 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District Board of Trustees derives its authority 
from the Education Code of the State of California.  The Board of Trustees is subject 
to provisions of the Constitution of the State of California and the laws of the State 
of California.  (Ed. Code, § 70902.) 
1.2 Board of Trustees members have authority only when acting as a Board of Trustees 
legally in session.  The Board of Trustees shall not be bound in any way by any 
statement or action on the part of any individual Board of Trustees member or 
employee, except when such statement or action is pursuant to specific instructions 
by the Board of Trustees. 
(Formerly P-3131)
