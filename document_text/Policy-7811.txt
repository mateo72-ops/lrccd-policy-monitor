INSTRUCTIONAL COMPUTER USE 
 
Purpose and Definition   P-7811 
Purpose and Definitions 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Purpose 
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District owns and operates a variety of 
computer systems and related equipment intended for instructional purposes. For 
purposes of these District Policies and Administrative Regulations, these systems 
and any part thereof shall be referred to as the Systems. The Systems are provided 
for the use of District students, faculty, and staff in support of the educational 
programs of the Colleges and are to be used for such related activities only. 
Commercial uses of the Systems are prohibited. All students, faculty and staff are 
responsible for ensuring that the Systems are used in an effective, efficient, 
ethical, and lawful manner. 
1.2 It is the intent of these policies and regulations to define acceptable use of the 
Systems. Unacceptable use is prohibited, and may be grounds for loss of 
computing privileges, as well as prosecution under Federal, State and local law. 
1.3 The Chancellor shall develop Administrative Regulations to implement this 
policy
