Development and Copyright of

P-8333 
Business Services 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Purpose  
 (Ed. Code 32360, Ed. Code 32361, Ed. Code 72207, Ed. Code 
81459) 
1.1 This policy establishes the Los Rios Community College District guidelines 
concerning the copyrighting, publishing and ownership of materials developed by 
employees of the District. 
1.2 The purpose of this policy is to encourage creativity, to protect developers of 
copyrighted materials and to protect the District. 
1.3 This policy shall be read in conjunction with applicable provisions of collective 
bargaining agreements. 
2.0 Regulations 
2.1 The Chancellor shall adopt appropriate regulations.
