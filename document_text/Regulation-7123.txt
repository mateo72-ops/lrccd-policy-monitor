INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAM 
 
Academic Calendar R-7123 
Instructional Program 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Authorization 
1.1 The Chancellor or designee shall chair a committee of campus administrators, 
selected students, an academic senate representative from each college and a 
representative from Los Rios College Federation of Teachers to develop a 
calendar for the academic year's operation. The calendar will stipulate starting 
dates for each semester, cut-off dates for admissions and registration, end-of-term 
dates and other necessary dates. 
1.2 The committee will develop the academic calendar, maintaining the statutory 
number of days during the regular academic year. 
1.3 A summer session calendar will be included. 
1.4 The committee will finalize the recommended calendar in sufficient time to allow 
representatives of management and of the bargaining units to meet on the calendar 
according to the labor contract before January 15.
