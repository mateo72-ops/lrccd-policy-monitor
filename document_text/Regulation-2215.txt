ADMISSIONS AND RECORDS 
 
Transcript of Records   R-2215 
Enrollment 1 of 1 
 
1.0 Cost of Transcripts 
1.1 Each student shall be entitled to two (2) free copies of the student’s transcript, 
after which a charge not to exceed actual cost of each transcript shall be levied. 
(Ed. Code 76223) 
1.2 A student may obtain an expedited transcript by paying a fee for that service. 
2.0 Transfer of Records 
2.1 Student records from a particular college within the District shall be available to 
all other colleges within the District.
