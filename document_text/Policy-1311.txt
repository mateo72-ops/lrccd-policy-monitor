1.0  Purpose  
1.1 The Los Rios Community College District intends to maintain good relations with all 
segments of the community it serves and to provide opportunities for effective 
communication between the District and the community.  This responsibility is 
shared equally by everyone working within the District. All staff are encouraged to 
participate in community, civic and professional organizations; to seek opportunities 
to speak before such organizations; to provide accurate information to interested 
community members about the colleges and programs of the District; and to 
encourage input from the community. 
1.2 Community Relations Objectives: 
1.2.1 To obtain and maintain community support for the colleges and their 
programs. 
1.2.2 To help ensure satisfactory enrollment levels at the colleges. 
1.2.3 To provide the public timely and accurate information about the programs, 
activities and policies of the colleges and the District. 
1.2.4 To keep college personnel aware of the changing educational needs of the 
community. 
1.2.5 To maintain and strengthen working relationships with other educational 
institutions. 
1.2.6 To maintain a favorable relationship with governmental entities. 
1.2.7 To encourage public use of the colleges as centers of educational and 
cultural activity. 
1.2.8 To involve the community in college programs and activities.  
1.2.9 To involve the community in decisions relating to significant construction 
or expansion of District facilities. 
1.3 Community Input 
1.3.1 The District may, where appropriate, cause to be formed an advisory 
group(s) consisting of members of the community.  An advisory group 
may be formed at the direction of the Board of Trustees or the Chancellor.  
The size and composition of any advisory group will vary depending upon 
the purpose of the group.
